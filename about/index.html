Choppy Bitcoin

Fly through Japanese candles. The price moves while you fly. Score is total net worth in BTC at the in-game price.

How to play

In-game cash, price and score are play money. They are not real bitcoin.

You are the ₿. Tap or press space to flap through the candle gaps. A wick liquidates you. The floor only counts when you fully leave the screen.

Candles pay cash. Buy BTC on the dip, sell on the rip. Score is play-money net worth in BTC at the live in-game price.

Bull pumps price. Bear dumps it.

Black swan is a black crash that dumps hard and stretches the bear. Halving is a fat bull. It sits at the top or just above Buy/Sell.

Cold storage saves a hit. Ten colds become one multisig life.

Laser eyes eat a bear and unlock a perk.
